# Code of conduct

SitRepHQ is a small project run in spare time. The expectations here are short
because they're simple.

## Expected

- Be respectful to everyone, whatever their experience level.
- Keep criticism about the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming otherwise.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change. Scope decisions aren't
  personal.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory jokes or language
- Sexual content or unwelcome attention
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Continuing an argument after being asked to stop

## Scope

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, and
anywhere else you're representing the project.
